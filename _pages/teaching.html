Teaching Philosophy

I believe teaching is not a side duty of academia but one of its core responsibilities, and I think it's a part of the job too many professors treat as an afterthought to research. My approach centers on hands-on, experimental learning: rather than just lecturing on theory, I design lab sessions where students build, test, and characterize real materials and structures themselves, since I've found that intuition sticks best when it's built with your own hands. I try to stay accessible outside the classroom through regular office hours, because the questions students are most hesitant to ask in front of a room are often the ones that matter most.
