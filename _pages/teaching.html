Teaching Philosophy

I believe teaching is one of the core responsibilities of academia. My approach centers on being flexible in learning tools, level of the student, and pace, since no two students arrive at a concept the same way. This comes largely from how I learn best myself: I often need to break a concept down to its simplest possible form before it clicks — I still remember needing to literally write out 0 × 1 = 0 to fully trust an index in the Kronecker delta during continuum mechanics. I try to teach the way I wish I’d been taught, starting from the ground level rather than assuming a shortcut everyone already sees.
