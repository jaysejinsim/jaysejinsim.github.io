I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Stanford University, where I work on magneto-mechanical and other electromagnetically-driven soft materials and metamaterials. My research focuses on designing and modeling active materials whose mechanical properties can be programmed and reconfigured on demand — for applications ranging from tunable acoustic and elastic wave control to soft robotics and minimally invasive medical devices.

My work sits at the intersection of solid mechanics, materials science, and functional design, combining theory, simulation, and experiment to understand how magnetic, structural, and material nonlinearities can be harnessed to create multifunctional systems.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to collaborate or just chat about research.
